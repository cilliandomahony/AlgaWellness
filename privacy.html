Alga Wellness Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Alga Wellness (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada-based business. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

1. Information we collect

When you join our waitlist, we may collect your first name and email address. We may also collect limited technical information about your visit to our website, depending on the website hosting, analytics and cookie tools we use.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Manage our waitlist and contact you about Alga Wellness’s launch.
  • Share product updates, launch announcements and relevant news, where you have consented to receive these communications.
  • Maintain and improve our website and services.

3. Email communications

We use MailerLite to manage our email subscriber list and send emails. MailerLite processes subscriber information on our behalf. You can read its Privacy Policy.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in our emails.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it with service providers, such as MailerLite, only as reasonably necessary to operate our waitlist, communicate with you and provide our services. Information may also be disclosed when required or permitted by law.

5. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against loss, unauthorized access, use or disclosure. Our service providers may process or store information outside British Columbia or Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. You may also request access to, or correction or deletion of, your personal information, subject to applicable law.
